Online services

How can we help?

Submit a non-emergency enquiry, contact NWAS roleplay staff, access patient-support information, or use the emergency call service for a medical incident happening now in-game.

Medical emergency happening now?

Use the roleplay 999 service to open a live mock call with an Emergency Operations Centre dispatcher. Ambulance, police and fire routing is available only within that emergency-call workflow.
