How can we help?

Report a non-emergency incident, contact Garda roleplay staff, access victim-support information, or use the emergency call service for an incident happening now in-game.

Crime happening now?

Use the roleplay 999 service to open a live mock call with a control-room operator. Garda, fire and ambulance routing is available only within that emergency-call workflow.
